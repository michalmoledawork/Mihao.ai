About Mihao.ai

Mihao.ai is a desktop application designed to help creators generate, organize, schedule, upload and monitor short-form video content across supported social platforms.

Features may include content generation, upload queues, channel automation, analytics, multilingual audio workflows and performance monitoring.

TikTok Integration

Mihao.ai may connect to a user's TikTok account only after the user explicitly authorizes access through TikTok's official login and authorization flow.

Mihao.ai uses authorized TikTok permissions only to provide features requested by the user, such as publishing content and reading video/account information where permitted.
