Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 September 2026

1. Overview

Mihao.ai is a desktop application for creating, managing, scheduling, publishing and monitoring short-form video content.

2. Information We Process

Mihao.ai may process account information, authentication tokens, channel identifiers, video metadata, publishing status and analytics data when you connect a supported social-media account.

3. TikTok Data

If you connect TikTok, Mihao.ai may receive information authorized by you through TikTok's official APIs, including basic account information, video information, publishing permissions and analytics fields made available by TikTok.

4. How Information Is Used

Information is used only to provide requested application features, including account connection, uploading, publishing, scheduling, queue management, analytics and automation.

5. Storage

Mihao.ai is primarily a desktop application. Credentials, access tokens and settings may be stored locally on the user's device as required for connected-account features.

6. Sharing

Mihao.ai does not sell personal information. Information may be transmitted to connected platforms such as TikTok only as required to perform actions requested by the user.

7. Data Removal

Users can disconnect supported accounts from Mihao.ai. Users may also revoke Mihao.ai's access from the relevant platform's account or application settings.

8. Security

Reasonable technical measures are used to protect locally stored credentials and authentication information. Users are responsible for securing their own computer and account credentials.

9. Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated as Mihao.ai features or platform integrations change.

10. Contact

For privacy questions, contact the Mihao.ai operator through the contact information associated with the application's official distribution or developer account.
